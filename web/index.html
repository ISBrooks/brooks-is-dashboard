Reports are built on demand from the commission engine's database on this server. A pay period appears in the list once its payroll run has produced a statement. Periods before go-live are marked PRE-GO-LIVE: the manual payroll sheet paid them.

Commission Lookup

Live view of the commission engine's database: every subscription's state, rate, contract value, vesting progress, and the plain-English why. Searches by PestRoutes account number, subscription number, or customer name.

Payroll Rules

Rendered live from the commission engine's own rulebook (COMMISSION_RULES.md) — the single source of truth. Every rule change lands in that file first, so what you read here is exactly what the engine pays by.

Commission Roster who the IS Commissions app actively calculates for

Inbound agents earn full ISA commission (9% / 5% alt-source / 3% no-card). Outbound agents are paid 3% by their own process; on shared accounts the inbound agent keeps the rate minus 3%. This list is what the commission engine will read at go-live — keep it current when agents start or leave.

Settings key agents, teams, and flags — the source of truth for every tab

Search any office-staff rep who's sold subscriptions (from PestRoutes) to add them. Click Edit on a tracked agent to change their team or flags. Key agents appear on IS #'s & Team Metrics; Power Score excludes outbound reps; Team drives the Team Metrics split.

Software Management every Brooks app and every data connection · checked live when you open this tab

Status is gathered on demand over a locked-down SSH key that can only run the read-only status agent on each server — nothing polls in the background. Click an app for its data connections and what the last run did.

Users who can sign in, and what they can see

People sign in with their email and a one-time code — there are no passwords to manage. Disabling a user signs them out immediately.
